REGULATION

Main Regulation

TCAR AIR Part – CAMO is the principal regulation governing CAMO certification in Thailand. It establishes the requirements that applicants and approved organisations must meet to obtain and maintain CAMO approval.

Acceptable Means of Compliance and Guidance Material (AMC/GM)

The AMC and GM to TCAR AIR Part-CAMO should be read in conjunction with the regulation. The AMC provides means accepted by CAAT for demonstrating compliance with TCAR AIR Part-CAMO, while the GM provides explanations and guidance to support the interpretation and application of the requirements.

For other related regulations, see the airworthiness regulation portal.

GUIDANCE MATERIAL

CAAT Guidance Materials provide non-binding explanations and practical guidance to help applicants and approval holders understand and apply the applicable requirements and AMC, including the development of their CAME and associated procedures.
